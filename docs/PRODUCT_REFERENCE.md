# Product reference and scope

Harbor GRC is an original local application, not Vanta or an affiliated product. Its scope takes inspiration from publicly described GRC workflows, not private source code, licensed policy text, or proprietary user-interface assets.

Vanta's public plans describe controls, policies, evidence, audit workflows, monitoring, personnel, assets, risk and questionnaire capabilities.[1] Its platform description also identifies integrations, framework customization, access reviews and vendor risk management.[2] Its third-party-risk product describes vendor inventory, assessments, inherent/residual scoring and continuing monitoring.[3]

## Local implementation targets

| Workflow | Local approach | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance frameworks and controls | Editable library, owners, implementation, cross-links, calculated readiness | Starter mappings are illustrative; no attestation or certification |
| Policies | Editable draft templates, versions, review and publish actions | Tailor to your organization; not legal advice |
| Evidence | Local files, integrity hashes, expiration dates and control links | Evidence is supplied by you, not automatically fetched from SaaS |
| Vendor management | Inventory, tiering, assessment notes, review dates, inherent/residual risk | No breach feed, automated discovery or threat intelligence |
| Risk management | Register, likelihood × impact, treatment, residual score, heatmap | Scores are program inputs, not external risk ratings |
| Audit preparation | Linked requests, evidence status and scoped export | Not an auditor portal or assurance opinion |
| Questionnaires | Question/answer editing and source-linked suggestions from published policies | Deterministic retrieval, not proprietary AI or automatic approval |
| Personnel, assets and access reviews | Local registers, owner-recorded attestations and review decisions | No device agent, identity verification, provisioning or actual access revocation |
| Trust center | Selected approved content, local preview and portable export | Not publicly hosted, access-gated, or automatically published |
| Monitoring | Checks against dates, assignments and recorded attributes | No connected cloud telemetry; missing data is not a passed check |
| Data ownership | SQLite, uploaded files, CSV import/export and backup | Single-user local trust boundary; backups are not encrypted by the app |

## Explicit non-parity

This app does not reproduce Vanta's proprietary AI, full supported-framework library, hundreds of cloud integrations, automatic security-evidence collection, real-time third-party intelligence, licensed content, auditor network, enterprise SSO/RBAC, external notifications, or hosted buyer-facing trust network. Integrations that are not implemented must be labeled unavailable rather than presented as connected.

Readiness reports describe recorded work. They do not prove compliance with a regulation or standard. Review the starter content and mappings with an appropriately qualified assessor before relying on them for an audit.
